Helmet Choice vs. Helmet Mandate â€” The Rider Rights Position

The AMA, MRF, and ABATE all share the same core position: the decision to wear a helmet should belong to the adult rider, not the government. This isn't an anti-safety argument â€” it's a freedom argument. Riders who choose to wear helmets (the vast majority do) should do so because they've made an informed choice, not because the state made it for them.

The AMA's Official Position The American Motorcyclist Association supports motorcycle rider education, training, and the right of motorcyclists to make their own decisions about helmet use. The AMA opposes laws that mandate helmet use for adult riders.
The Federal Pressure Track Federal highway funding incentives have historically been used to pressure states into universal helmet laws. The NHTSA continues to advocate for universal coverage nationally, but no federal mandate has been enacted.
