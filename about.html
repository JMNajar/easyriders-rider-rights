What This Is

The Easyriders Rider Rights Intelligence Center

This is a real-time policy intelligence platform built for the Easyriders Magazine community. It tracks the legislation, regulations, and political forces that shape the rights of American motorcyclists — across three issue areas that matter most to riders: the right to ride without government interference, the right to repair and maintain your own machine, and the ongoing fight over helmet choice.

Most riders don't have time to monitor 50 state legislatures, watch the Federal Register, and follow congressional committee activity. This platform does it automatically — aggregating data daily, surfacing what's moving, and translating policy language into plain English briefings that tell you what it means for you on the road.

It is always free. It is built to be trusted. Every piece of information is sourced from primary government sources, the AMA, the MRF, or major motorcycling publications — and every source is cited.

Keith BallEditor, Easyriders Magazine

Plain-English briefings are generated using the Anthropic Claude API. Briefings summarize the policy significance of each story for motorcyclists in terms of impact, timeline, and recommended action. Raw data is always shown alongside briefings so readers can verify the source.

This platform does not editorialize about policy positions. It presents what legislators are doing and what advocacy organizations are saying — and gives riders the information they need to form their own view and take action if they choose.
