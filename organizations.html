Independent Motorcycle Aftermarket
IMA — Operated under the MRF
imamembers.org
IMA
The IMA is the only national organization specifically focused on protecting the rights of independent motorcycle dealers, custom bike builders, aftermarket parts manufacturers, and enthusiasts to repair, modify, and maintain motorcycles using any qualified shop or parts supplier. It operates within the MRF's lobbying framework, giving it a direct congressional advocacy channel that most trade groups lack.
Right to Repair — primary mission: protecting your right to have your bike worked on by any qualified shop, not just a franchised dealer
ICE Engine Protection — fighting EPA and state-level regulations designed to phase out internal combustion engines post-2035
Independent Dealer Survival — franchised dealers rarely service bikes over 10 years old; independent shops fill the gap and must be protected
Custom Builder Rights — opposing regulatory frameworks that would make custom and modified motorcycles non-compliant
Aftermarket Parts — protecting the legal sale and installation of aftermarket components by any qualified technician
How to Engage

Join or learn more at imamembers.org. Membership is open to dealers, builders, parts suppliers, and enthusiasts. The IMA sends legislative alerts through the MRF network when action is needed on repair and ICE protection bills.

State ABATE Chapters — Key States

Supporting HB 1927 (lane filtering) and monitoring state right-to-repair bills. Large chapter network across all major metro areas.

Florida

ABATE of Florida

Monitors helmet law rollback efforts and lane filtering legislation. Hosts Biketoberfest liaison activities and state Bike Week advocacy.

Ohio

ABATE of Ohio

Actively lobbying for HB 244 (adult helmet choice repeal). One of the most organized state chapters in the Midwest.

Pennsylvania

ABATE of Pennsylvania

Long-running efforts to restore full adult helmet choice. Pennsylvania's partial law still requires coverage for younger riders and those without experience + insurance.

New York

ABATE of New York

Operates under a universal helmet mandate. Focused on preventing expansion of restrictions and monitoring lane filtering bill progress.
